**Kamurafy 0.3: why is your Mac slow, now in English and Spanish, with dark mode.**

### New
- **Speed › "Why is my Mac slow?"**: one click ranks what is slowing your Mac down right now (memory pressure, maxed-out apps, browsers with too many tabs, Spotlight / Time Machine / iCloud / Photos in the background, heat throttling, Low Power Mode, a nearly full disk, days without a restart, login items), each with its fix.
- **Make it fly**: after a scan, one button does the safe fixes at once, only after you review and confirm.
- **Watchdog**: a notification when an app stays maxed out for 10 minutes or memory hits the limit, with "Don't warn me about this one".
- **Dark mode** ("notebook at night") and **English, Portuguese and Spanish**, following your macOS settings or your choice in Settings.
- **New-version alert**, off by default (it is the only thing that talks to the internet).
- New menu: Home, Speed, Battery, Storage, Uninstall, Live. Screens with much less text.

### Fixed
- Processes started from Terminal or a code editor (node, python, a build) now show up with their own name instead of being added to Terminal, so closing one no longer closes the whole Terminal.

### Install
Download `Kamurafy.dmg` below and drag it to Applications. It is not notarized yet: on macOS 15+, open it once, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. Check the download with the `.sha256` file.
